# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What's expected

- Be patient with people who don't code. This project exists for them; treating
  them as the problem defeats the point.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Accept that "I got confused by this wording" is valid, complete feedback. It
  does not need to be reproduced with a stack trace.
- Korean and English are both welcome. Don't correct someone's grammar unless
  they asked.

## What isn't

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Dismissing someone for not knowing Git, or for being new.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space this project
uses.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory on GitHub, or contact the maintainer directly
through their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer only.

Whoever receives a report will respond as fast as they can, keep the reporter's
identity private, and act — with a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent one,
depending on what happened.

Maintainers are held to this too. If a maintainer breaks it, say so publicly in an
issue; the project is small enough that sunlight is the mechanism.
